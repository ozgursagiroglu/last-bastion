Last Bastion — a free browser tower defense game

Last Bastion is a free tower defense game for the browser: 10 levels on four themed maps, five towers with three upgrade levels each, six enemy types up to the Orc Warlord boss, and three difficulties. It was built by parallel AI agents with arbab, and every pixel is rendered with the ExodeUI runtime.

How to play

  • Click a stone pad to build a tower.
  • Click a tower to upgrade or sell it.
  • Press START to send the first wave.
  • Enemies that reach the castle cost lives.
